# MQ2 partial-exam study videos

Site: https://sergiogd112.github.io/mq2-study-videos/

Twelve narrated lectures (about five hours) for the four MQ2 partial exams of the MUEA master's degree
(ESEIAAT, UPC, 2026–27): navigation systems (TASN), propulsion (PROP), RF systems (RF) and aerospace
vehicles (VA: pneumatic and hydraulic systems). Each video has a page with chapter navigation, subtitles and
notes that follow it chapter by chapter: bullet points, worked solutions, key equations in LaTeX, interactive
and animated figures, and the full narration.

PROP, RF and VA also have Spanish versions (nine lectures, about 3.8 hours, `index-es.html`): Spanish narration,
subtitles, notes and figure texts. Each lecture page links to its counterpart in the other language.

Each exam also has a formula sheet (`formulas-<subject>.html`): LaTeX on the web, a two-page A4 PDF and an
ASCII plain-text export in `sheets/`.

Unofficial study material prepared by a student for exam revision. The narration is synthetic. Numbers were
checked against the course solutions where they exist, but the course material and the lecturers are the
reference. The figures in the notes are original schematic drawings; some slides in the videos reproduce
figures from course documents, credited in the sources of each chapter.

This is a static site generated by `site/build_site.py` in the (separate) video project; the math is
pre-rendered with KaTeX.
