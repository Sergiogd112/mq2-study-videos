MQ2 - VA FORMULA SHEET
======================
220306 Vehicles Aeroespacials
A320 pneumatic (ATA 36) and hydraulic up to Green (ATA 29): numbers and logic

Scope: Pneumatic system (whole deck) and hydraulic system up to the Green system included. The quiz
tests one number or one cause and effect per statement, so this sheet is mostly numbers, defaults
and logic.
Rules: Quiz format: circle the true (or false) statements; older papers ask to explain the TLT, TCT
or the crossbleed.
Web version: https://sergiogd112.github.io/mq2-study-videos/formulas-va.html

Notation: ^ power, sqrt() square root, log = log10, ln natural log, _ subscript, +- plus or minus,
~ approximately, -> leads to, <=> equivalent, int integral, sum summation.
Greek letters are spelled out (gamma, eta, pi, ...).

1. PNEUMATIC: SOURCES, USERS, CONVENTIONS
-----------------------------------------
  Item                        | Value
  ----------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Engine bleed (high stages)  | 50-410 psi, 180-540 C (idle -> cruise)
  Regulated bleed (reference) | 44 psi (PRV), 200 C (precooler loop)
  Bleed ports                 | IP = HP stage 5 (normal, via IPCV); HP = HP stage 9 (idle, via HPV)
  APU bleed                   | ground and flight up to 20 000 ft; feeds the LEFT side; valve opens at 95 % APU speed
  Ground cart                 | lower LEFT fuselage connector, two-flap check valve; feeds the LEFT side
  Hydraulic reservoirs        | 50 psi, from HP stage 8 of ENGINE 1 (deck drawing says 9: wrong)
  MEL deferral                | B 3 days, C 10 days, D 120 days
  Numbering                   | odd = left (1), even = right (2)
- Users: packs and pressurisation (ATA 21), engine start, wing anti-ice (from slat 3, piccolo duct)
  and engine anti-ice (ATA 30), hydraulic reservoirs (ATA 29), potable water (ATA 38), aft cargo
  heating (engine 1).

2. ENGINE BLEED VALVES
----------------------
  Valve           | Without pressure    | Numbers                                                     | Commanded by
  ----------------+---------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------
  IPCV (stage 5)  | check valve         | opens when stage 5 > downstream                             | pressure difference
  HPV (stage 9)   | spring CLOSED       | >8 psi opens; regulates 36 +- 4 psi; CLOSED if stage 5 > 36 | pneumatic, slaved to PRV chamber C
  PRV             | spring CLOSED       | >=8 psi fully open; regulates 44 +- 4 psi; fuse 450 C       | TLT sense line (solenoid: BMC / EEC)
  OPV             | spring OPEN         | starts closing 74 psi, closed 90 psi (deck 73.8-89.9)       | own pistons
  FAV (fan air)   | spring CLOSED       | loop holds 200 +- 15 C                                      | TCT signal air
  X-BLEED         | electric, 2 motors  | AUTO: same position as the APU bleed valve                  | motor 1 BMCs (AUTO), motor 2 manual
  APU bleed valve | solenoid controlled | + check valve against reverse flow                          | BMC via ECB
- No bleed at all: HPV, PRV and FAV closed, only the OPV open. PRV closed => HPV closed, NOT the
  reverse (cruise: HPV closed while the PRV regulates). Valves can only reduce pressure, never add
  it.

3. WHO REGULATES AT EACH POWER (STAGE-5 PRESSURE P5)
----------------------------------------------------
  p5                     | IPCV                    | HPV               | PRV                  | System gets
  -----------------------+-------------------------+-------------------+----------------------+----------------------
  < 36 psi (idle)        | closed (stage 9 higher) | regulates 36 +- 4 | fully open           | 36 psi
  36-44 psi              | open                    | fully CLOSED      | fully open           | p5
  > 44 psi (cruise)      | open                    | closed            | regulates 44 +- 4    | 44 psi
  PRV fails high         |                         |                   | BMC closes at 57 psi | 0 (bleed off)
  ... and solenoid fails |                         |                   |                      | OPV limits: 74-90 psi

4. CLOSING THE PRV (AND WITH IT THE HPV)
----------------------------------------
- Manual: ENG BLEED pb OFF, ENG FIRE pb pushed (both energise the TLT solenoid).
- BMC (electrical, TLT solenoid): > 57 psi after the PRV (Pr transmitter); > 257 C after the
  precooler; leak detected; engine START sequence of that engine; APU BLEED selected (APU priority).
- Pneumatic, inside the TLT: REVERSE flow (diaphragm: before-PRV vs after-precooler taps) -> closes;
  over-temperature 235-245 C -> partial venting, PRV THROTTLES (does not shut).
- PRV itself: upstream < 8 psi -> spring closes it; thermal fuse melts at 450 C.

5. TEMPERATURE LADDER AFTER THE PRECOOLER
-----------------------------------------
  Temperature | Device                                    | Action
  ------------+-------------------------------------------+----------------------------------------------
  200 +- 15 C | TCT + FAV (pneumatic, continuous)         | fan air through the precooler regulates
  235-245 C   | TLT probe                                 | bleeds the PRV sense line: PRV throttles back
  257 C       | temperature sensor -> BMC -> TLT solenoid | PRV + HPV closed, ECAM caution
  450 C       | PRV thermal fuse                          | vents the sense line: last resort
- TCT: probe after the precooler (tube expands more than the inner rod). T < 200 C: vent closed,
  chamber A full, needle valve closed, NO air to the FAV (FAV closed). T >= 200 C: vent opens,
  needle valve opens, air to the FAV: proportional. Temperature is controlled ONLY by TCT, FAV,
  sense line and precooler (not by the BMC).
- Precooler (in the pylon): hot bleed inside the tubes, fan air outside then overboard: heat
  exchange, no mixing.

6. CROSSBLEED, APU AND ENGINE START
-----------------------------------
- X-BLEED AUTO opens (motor 1, either BMC) if: APU bleed valve OPEN + no ENG FIRE pb + no leak (a
  leak during a start sequence is tolerated until the sequence ends). APU valve closes -> X-BLEED
  closes. OPEN/SHUT: motor 2, fixed, independent of the APU.
- APU bleed valve opens if: APU BLEED pb ON, APU FIRE pb not pushed, no APU-duct leak (tolerated on
  ground until engines start), no APU shut-down signal, APU speed stabilised at 95 %. APU BLEED ON
  closes both engine PRVs.
- Start with APU air: APU BLEED ON -> APU valve and (AUTO) X-BLEED open -> START valve -> air
  starter -> N2, N1. During its start the engine's PRV is held closed.
- ENG FIRE pb: TLT solenoid -> PRV + HPV of that engine closed, X-BLEED (AUTO) cannot open;
  hydraulic fire valve closes (ENG 1 Green, ENG 2 Yellow). APU FIRE pb: BMC refuses APU bleed. Two
  agents per engine, one for the APU.

7. COCKPIT AND COMPUTERS
------------------------
- Overhead: ENG BLEED dark = normal, white OFF, amber FAULT; APU BLEED blue ON; X-BLEED
  AUTO/OPEN/SHUT. Dark cockpit: a light = abnormal or non-standard.
- ECAM: class 1 RED (immediate action), class 2 AMBER (no immediate action), class 3 maintenance
  only (MCDU). BMC 1/2 identical; SDAC -> FWC (alerts) + DMC (displays); CFDIU faults; EIU <- ECU;
  ECB (APU).

8. HYDRAULIC: THREE SYSTEMS
---------------------------
  System           | Pumps                                    | Notes
  -----------------+------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------
  GREEN (main)     | EDP on ENG 1                             | most users, biggest reservoir; fills ALL reservoirs (service panel)
  YELLOW           | EDP on ENG 2 + electric pump + hand pump | hand pump: cargo doors without power
  BLUE (emergency) | electric pump + RAT                      | RAT also drives the CSM/G
- All 3000 psi, fully independent (own reservoir, pumps, pipes). PTU = hydraulic motor + pump on one
  shaft: transfers POWER Green <-> Yellow (no fluid) when dp > 500 psi, pb AUTO. PTU inhibited
  during engine start and cargo-door operation with the Yellow electric pump (and pb OFF).
- Ailerons: Blue + Green. Elevators: left Green + Blue, right Blue + Yellow. Green users: gear and
  doors, nose-wheel steering, normal brakes, reverser ENG 1, flap/slat motors, THS, rudder, yaw
  damper, spoilers, aileron, elevator. Yellow: alternate brakes, reverser ENG 2, cargo doors. Blue:
  emergency generator, slats.

9. HYDRAULIC NUMBERS (GREEN)
----------------------------
  Item                     | Value
  -------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------
  System pressure          | 3000 psi (EDP variable displacement, swashplate)
  PTU threshold            | dp > 500 psi
  Reservoir air            | 50 psi (3.5 bar); LO AIR PRESS < 22 psi (Green, Yellow), < 30 psi (Blue)
  Green reservoir          | 18 l max, 14 l normal, LOW LEVEL at 3 l (capacitive sensor)
  Pump LO pressure         | < 1740 psi
  Manifold pressure switch | 1450 psi (to ELAC, SEC, FAC, BSCU, ECAM): normal brakes lost below
  Relief valve             | opens 3500 psi, closes 3200 psi
  Filters                  | HP and case drain clog at 87 psi dp; return filter 29 psi
  Accumulator              | N2 1885 psi at 20 C, 1 l (steel + Kevlar)
  Pipe band number         | 1 Green, 2 Blue, 3 Yellow; black dots = pressure line
* accumulator (isothermal N2, ABSOLUTE pressures = gauge + 14.7 psi): p V = p0 V0;
      V_fluid = V0 (1 - p0,abs/p_abs) = 1 (1 - 1899.7/3014.7) = 0.37 l
- Priority valve: low pressure -> cuts heavy users (gear, doors, steering, flap/slat motors) to keep
  the primary flight controls. Leak-measurement valve: normally OPEN, isolates the primary flight
  control users (tail: THS motor, rudder, yaw damper; each wing: aileron servo, spoilers 1 and 5)
  for internal-leak tests.
- Fire valve: LH pylon, electric motor, closed by ENG 1 FIRE pb. ECAM HYD: pump in line green =
  delivering; LO amber < 1740 psi; system label amber when low; RAT automatic on electrical loss or
  RAT MAN ON (guarded).
