No bleed at all: HPV, PRV and FAV closed, only the OPV open. PRV closed ⇒ HPV closed, NOT the reverse (cruise: HPV closed while the PRV regulates). Valves can only reduce pressure, never add it.

Closing the PRV (and with it the HPV)

Manual: ENG BLEED pb OFF, ENG FIRE pb pushed (both energise the TLT solenoid).

BMC (electrical, TLT solenoid): > 57 psi after the PRV (Pr transmitter); > 257 °C after the precooler; leak detected; engine START sequence of that engine; APU BLEED selected (APU priority).

Pneumatic, inside the TLT: REVERSE flow (diaphragm: before-PRV vs after-precooler taps) → closes; over-temperature 235-245 °C → partial venting, PRV THROTTLES (does not shut).

PRV itself: upstream < 8 psi → spring closes it; thermal fuse melts at 450 °C.

TCT: probe after the precooler (tube expands more than the inner rod). T < 200 °C: vent closed, chamber A full, needle valve closed, NO air to the FAV (FAV closed). T ≥ 200 °C: vent opens, needle valve opens, air to the FAV: proportional. Temperature is controlled ONLY by TCT, FAV, sense line and precooler (not by the BMC).

Precooler (in the pylon): hot bleed inside the tubes, fan air outside then overboard: heat exchange, no mixing.

Crossbleed, APU and engine start

X-BLEED AUTO opens (motor 1, either BMC) if: APU bleed valve OPEN + no ENG FIRE pb + no leak (a leak during a start sequence is tolerated until the sequence ends). APU valve closes → X-BLEED closes. OPEN/SHUT: motor 2, fixed, independent of the APU.

APU bleed valve opens if: APU BLEED pb ON, APU FIRE pb not pushed, no APU-duct leak (tolerated on ground until engines start), no APU shut-down signal, APU speed stabilised at 95 %. APU BLEED ON closes both engine PRVs.

Start with APU air: APU BLEED ON → APU valve and (AUTO) X-BLEED open → START valve → air starter → N2, N1. During its start the engine's PRV is held closed.

ENG FIRE pb: TLT solenoid → PRV + HPV of that engine closed, X-BLEED (AUTO) cannot open; hydraulic fire valve closes (ENG 1 Green, ENG 2 Yellow). APU FIRE pb: BMC refuses APU bleed. Two agents per engine, one for the APU.

Cockpit and computers

Overhead: ENG BLEED dark = normal, white OFF, amber FAULT; APU BLEED blue ON; X-BLEED AUTO/OPEN/SHUT. Dark cockpit: a light = abnormal or non-standard.

ECAM: class 1 RED (immediate action), class 2 AMBER (no immediate action), class 3 maintenance only (MCDU). BMC 1/2 identical; SDAC → FWC (alerts) + DMC (displays); CFDIU faults; EIU ← ECU; ECB (APU).

SystemPumpsNotes

All 3000 psi, fully independent (own reservoir, pumps, pipes). PTU = hydraulic motor + pump on one shaft: transfers POWER Green ↔ Yellow (no fluid) when dp > 500 psi, pb AUTO. PTU inhibited during engine start and cargo-door operation with the Yellow electric pump (and pb OFF).

Ailerons: Blue + Green. Elevators: left Green + Blue, right Blue + Yellow. Green users: gear and doors, nose-wheel steering, normal brakes, reverser ENG 1, flap/slat motors, THS, rudder, yaw damper, spoilers, aileron, elevator. Yellow: alternate brakes, reverser ENG 2, cargo doors. Blue: emergency generator, slats.

Priority valve: low pressure → cuts heavy users (gear, doors, steering, flap/slat motors) to keep the primary flight controls. Leak-measurement valve: normally OPEN, isolates the primary flight control users (tail: THS motor, rudder, yaw damper; each wing: aileron servo, spoilers 1 and 5) for internal-leak tests.

Fire valve: LH pylon, electric motor, closed by ENG 1 FIRE pb. ECAM HYD: pump in line green = delivering; LO amber < 1740 psi; system label amber when low; RAT automatic on electrical loss or RAT MAN ON (guarded).
